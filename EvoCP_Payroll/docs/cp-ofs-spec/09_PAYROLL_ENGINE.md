# Payroll Engine — Final V2

## 1. Source

Payroll consumes `payroll_eligible` worker earnings not already included in another payroll period, plus approved structured payroll components. It never calculates production pay by matching employee names.

## 2. Period

A payroll period has explicit start/end date and lifecycle:
`draft -> calculated -> reviewed -> approved -> paid -> closed`.

Do not hard-code weekly payroll.

## 3. Component semantic model

Each component stores a positive `amount_minor` and:
- `component_type`
- `component_effect = earning|deduction|employer_cost|informational`
- source lineage
- optional rate basis / basis points
- whether manually introduced
- approval reference when required

Avoid using an arbitrary signed number as the main business semantic.

## 4. Totals

`base_earnings = sum(eligible worker earnings)`

`gross_compensation = base_earnings + genuine_bonus + positive_compensation_adjustments`

`net_cash_pay = gross_compensation - employee_deductions`

`employer_cost` is computed from compensation and employer-side costs according to configured policy. Employee receivable/debt repayments reduce cash paid but do not reduce already-recognized labor cost.

## 5. Worker-cost accounting

Where direct worker cost was recognized when earning became finance-recognized:

- Debit Worker/Service Cost
- Credit Worker Payable

Payroll payment clears Worker Payable. It must not post the same Worker Cost again.

## 6. Advance example

Worker payable: Rp1,000,000  
Advance repayment: Rp200,000  
Cash transfer: Rp800,000

Payroll settlement accounting conceptually clears:
- Rp200,000 Worker Payable against Employee Receivable
- Rp800,000 Worker Payable against Bank/Cash

Labor cost remains Rp1,000,000.

## 7. Maintenance / laundry

If retained as a business rule, it is a once-per-applicable-period structured component generated by policy, not multiplied by order count. Override requires reason/permission/approval according to policy.

## 8. Subscription

Structured deduction with source/policy lineage. Do not merge it into an unexplained “other deduction”.

## 9. Percentage withholding

Rule must explicitly declare basis such as:
`order_gross|allocation_gross|worker_earnings|payroll_gross`.

## 10. Genuine bonus and legacy BON

Production `bonus` means positive compensation. Legacy `BON/KASBON/warung` labels are migrated only after their true meaning is mapped; do not interpret ambiguous legacy text as a bonus automatically.

## 11. Penalty

A penalty is a structured deduction with reason/evidence and approval as required. It cannot be created by arbitrary silent editing of net pay.

## 12. Advance/debt repayment protection

Repayment cannot exceed current outstanding. Final repayment record is created/settled consistently when payroll is marked paid.

## 13. Calculation snapshot

Calculation creates a deterministic hash from all relevant inputs and versions. Review stores/references that snapshot. Approval must fail with `PAYROLL_CHANGED_SINCE_REVIEW` if inputs changed.

## 14. Paid immutability

Paid/closed payroll cannot be recalculated in place. Corrections use:
- approved adjustment;
- correction payroll;
- reversal/settlement correction where accounting requires it.

## 15. Negative pay

Unexpected negative net cash pay is a blocking payroll issue in V1. A future carry-forward-debt policy must be explicit rather than allowing a negative bank transfer.

## 16. Payroll versus attendance/workforce

Attendance, leave, shift, and work session data do not automatically change production-share earnings. If an approved workforce policy produces a payroll event (e.g. genuine overtime or fixed-salary adjustment), that event becomes a structured payroll component.

## 17. Payroll privacy

Workers may read only their own payroll/earnings/debt data unless another role grants broader access. Finance/team permissions must not leak payroll through search, analytics, or exports.

## 18. Golden regression

Earnings Rp1,000,000 + genuine bonus Rp100,000 - maintenance Rp50,000 - advance repayment Rp200,000 - penalty Rp25,000 = net cash pay Rp825,000.

Compensation/labor cost remains Rp1,100,000 before employer-specific costs; the Rp200,000 advance repayment is balance-sheet settlement, not reduced labor cost.
