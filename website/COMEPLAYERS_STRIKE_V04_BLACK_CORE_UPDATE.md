# ComePlayers Strike v0.4 — Black Core Update

## Gameplay
- Zombie Adventure expanded from 5 to 7 chapters.
- Added checkpoint revives, emergency supply cache, dual generator objectives, reactor guardians, and extraction sequence.
- Added Crawler and Screamer infected archetypes.
- Outbreak Colossus now has three phases, reinforcement summons, shockwaves, and bio-plasma volleys.
- Added kill combo tracking and temporary Overdrive damage boosts.

## World and objects
- Added raised service catwalk, perimeter fences, generators, animated pickups, toxic waste hazards, and explosive barrels.
- Explosive barrels damage nearby infected, can chain-react, and are represented on the collision/raycast system.
- Added health, armor, ammunition, and Overdrive field pickups.

## Controls
- E: interact with objectives.
- Shoot red explosive barrels for area damage.
- Pickups are collected automatically by walking near them.
