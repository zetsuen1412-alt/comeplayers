COMEPLAYERS MARBLE V90 - BANKRUPTCY RESCUE & LIQUIDATION

1. Rent payments no longer declare bankruptcy merely because cash reaches zero. Bankruptcy now occurs only when the full rent remains unpaid after every available rescue action.
2. Added automatic asset liquidation when a player cannot cover rent from cash.
3. Building levels are sold first at 70% of their original construction cost.
4. Land is returned to the bank at 60% of its purchase price only if building sales are still insufficient.
5. Liquidation order uses strategic preservation scoring to protect completed Color Monopoly groups, Tourism progress, Landmarks, protected cities, and high-rent properties as long as possible.
6. Payment decisions now report cash before payment, recovered liquidation value, asset actions, unpaid balance, survival status, and bankruptcy state.
7. The 3D board plays auction rings, recovery beams, and bank-seal effects on downgraded or repossessed properties.
8. HUD includes a permanent Rescue Economy rail and detailed automatic-liquidation reporting in the rent window.
9. Takeover availability is recalculated after rent and liquidation, preventing insolvent players from taking over property.
10. Fixed the match result snapshot duplicate propertyValue field and corrected the previous cash-zero bankruptcy condition.
11. V89 Special District Strategy, V88 Fortune Signal, V87 Development Planner, V86 Color Monopoly, classic victory conditions, career, ranked, replay, dice control, and authoritative multiplayer remain included.
12. Save schema remains unchanged and V89 local save data stays compatible.
