# ComePlayers Marble V105 – Video Reference Gameplay Remake

V105 uses the supplied `monopoli1(1).mp4` gameplay video as the primary presentation reference.

## Camera
- Fixed isometric match camera.
- Full board remains visible during normal turns.
- Very small camera bias for dice, movement, landing, cards, and property events.
- No per-tile chase camera.

## Board presentation
- Dark wooden tabletop.
- Ivory property track with saturated district bands.
- Neutral gray center arena for dice and event cards.
- Compact cash stacks around the board.
- Large decorative world objects and billboards removed from the active match view.

## HUD
- Four compact player cards remain at the corners.
- Compact top status bar and turn strip.
- Smaller turn banner, dice result, and arrival card.
- Dice parity/range controls remain in logic but are hidden from the normal match view.
- Advanced systems remain in the optional INFO panel.

## Gameplay presentation
- Route preview simplified to low-profile dots.
- Active tile highlight reduced to one restrained ring.
- Landing effect reduced to a short ring and six sparks.
- Property models redesigned as readable villa, building, hotel, and landmark silhouettes.

Open the project with Cocos Creator 3.8.8. The correct build shows `VIDEO REFERENCE • V105` in the match HUD.
