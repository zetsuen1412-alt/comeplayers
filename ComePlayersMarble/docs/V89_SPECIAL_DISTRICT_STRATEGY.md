# V89 Special District Strategy

V89 deepens the classic marble-game decision layer after landing on special tiles.

## Game Center risk lanes
- Safe Signal: low entry and low reward.
- Pro Signal: balanced entry and reward.
- Jackpot Signal: high entry and high reward.
- A protected reserve floor prevents normal lanes from consuming the player's final cash.
- A recovery signal remains available to low-balance players.

## World Travel intelligence
Every destination forecasts ownership state, immediate expense, remaining reserve, reserve risk, Color Monopoly completion, and Triple/Line/Tourism progress.

## Olympic host forecast
Every eligible city displays current rent, projected rent, percentage gain, resulting multiplier, and threat tier.

## Presentation
Every special district uses an original ComePlayers 3D monument and the HUD keeps a live Special District status rail.

The online authoritative server remains the source of truth for network matches. Local strategic previews do not overwrite server snapshots.
