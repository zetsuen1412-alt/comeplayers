# V77 Classic Parity Overhaul

V77 concentrates on visual composition rather than adding unrelated systems. The target is the dense and readable presentation of classic PC marble games while retaining original ComePlayers branding, characters, maps, and assets.

## Board presentation

- Warm mahogany tabletop and layered gold frame.
- Ivory property-card surfaces with district color bands.
- Rectangular edge tiles and enlarged corner spaces.
- Recessed dark center field, original CP emblem, card decks, dice platform, and original robot spectators.
- Camera framing tuned to keep the complete board visible while using more of the screen.

## HUD presentation

- Compact corner player cards replace large dashboard blocks.
- Rank medallions, portrait frames, cash/asset/city/building data, and monopoly progress remain visible.
- Purple round ROLL control, round timer, classic top status bar, and bottom system ticker.
- Parchment-style property and fortune windows.

## Lobby presentation

- Brighter blue classic client frames and gold highlights.
- Larger Alexia artwork in the equipped-character panel.
- Room browser, profile hub, event strip, notice strip, and bottom navigation remain functional.

## Compatibility

V77 does not change the local save schema or authoritative network protocol. V76 saves, replay data, ranked data, and career progression remain compatible.
