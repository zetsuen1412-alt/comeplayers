# V92 Match Legacy Awards

V92 turns the final result into a readable match recap rather than a plain ranking list.

## Awards

- Match MVP: official winner and highest-impact finisher.
- City Tycoon: most owned properties.
- Master Builder: highest building count.
- Cash Reserve: most remaining cash.
- Iron Survivor: strongest solvent finisher.

## Performance grade

Every player receives a 0-100 score and S/A/B/C/D grade using rank, net-worth share, city control, building development, survival, and victory.

## Presentation

The HUD shows four classic client-style award cards. The 3D board adds animated rings, crowns, and beacons around honored players before the result panel appears.
