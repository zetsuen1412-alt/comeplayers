# V81 Classic World Parity

V81 addresses the remaining prototype appearance by changing the whole match composition rather than adding another small overlay.

## Visual direction
The board uses a bright arcade-blue outer cabinet, ivory property track, gold dividers, saturated district colors, and a sky-world center. The central field is intentionally dense: cloud clusters, an enlarged globe, orbit rings, a skyline ring, two chance decks, and a separate dice stage.

## Property presentation
Property ownership and development are designed to read from the complete-board camera. Owner flags rotate by board side. Static district miniatures are larger, while dynamic villa, building, hotel, and landmark stages occupy more vertical space and use stronger window lighting.

## Camera
The main camera uses a narrower field of view and a more top-down angle. This keeps the whole board visible while making the track and property development visually dominant.

## HUD
The match HUD and authoritative multiplayer overlay use a bright blue/gold client palette. Player cards and the bottom dice dock are smaller than V80 so that the board remains the primary object on screen.

## Compatibility
V81 does not change local save schemas, ranked records, career records, replay data, dice-control requests, victory logic, or authoritative multiplayer protocols.
