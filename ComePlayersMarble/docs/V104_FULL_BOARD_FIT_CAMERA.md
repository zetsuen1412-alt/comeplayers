# V104 Full Board-Fit Camera

The V103 camera was more comfortable than earlier builds, but still framed too tightly during turn-focus and landing events. V104 addresses that by:

- moving the overview camera higher and farther away,
- increasing readable board coverage,
- weakening tile-follow bias,
- keeping event presentation readable without extreme zoom,
- preserving the classic isometric angle.

The target outcome is a camera closer to a static board game presentation than a chase camera.
