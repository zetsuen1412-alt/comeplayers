# V84 Living Board Director

V84 improves match readability and board life without changing economy or network authority.

## Presentation systems
- Active player spotlight follows the current piece.
- Owned properties gain animated district rings, street lamps, and status crowns.
- The center diorama gains a match-director hub with player beacons.
- Landing events use a two-stage camera settle and crown burst.
- The HUD displays live turn order.

## Compatibility
The save schema, authoritative multiplayer messages, replay format, ranked progression, career progression, and classic victory logic remain unchanged from V83.
