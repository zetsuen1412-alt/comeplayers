# V85 Classic Broadcast & City Economy

V85 improves board readability and classic match presentation without changing match authority or save schemas.

## Board presentation
- Active-turn broadcast satellites.
- Route step gates and destination guide fins.
- Outer arena broadcast towers.
- Property rent meters and economy orbiters.

## HUD
The City Intel rail reports the active tile, property owner, building level, current rent, purchase price, and base rent.

## Compatibility
V84 saves, server-authoritative multiplayer, replays, ranked progression, career progression, dice control, and classic victory rules remain compatible.
