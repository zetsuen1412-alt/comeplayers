# V80 Full Classic Visual Remake

V80 focuses on visual density, board readability, and classic PC game-client composition. The release keeps original ComePlayers art direction and does not bundle third-party game assets.

## Board
The procedural board now uses a central world showcase, enlarged corner spaces, deeper property cards, three group pips, and miniature district silhouettes. Property development has clearer villa, building, hotel, and landmark stages.

## Camera and pacing
Camera presets are closer and lower while retaining complete-board visibility. Dice, movement, tile resolution, special events, and victory presentations hold longer for readability.

## HUD and lobby
The match HUD adds mode and live-threat rails plus compact status strips. The lobby adds a persistent eight-entry game-client navigation dock and V80 world-map room labeling.

## Compatibility
V80 does not change local save schemas, online profile data, ranked records, replay data, or authoritative multiplayer protocols.
