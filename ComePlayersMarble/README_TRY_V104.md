# V104 – Full Board-Fit Comfort Camera

This update pulls the gameplay camera farther back so the whole board remains readable.

## Main fixes
- Raised and pulled back the overview camera.
- Reduced target bias for turn start, movement, landing, and property focus.
- Reduced over-zoom during dice, card, and result presentation.
- Kept the V102 clean HUD and V103 comfort camera flow.
- Updated UI labels to V104.

## Expected result
- The board should stay visible even when a player turn starts.
- The camera should no longer crop one corner of the map during normal play.
- The camera still gives mild focus for events, but does not hide most of the board.
