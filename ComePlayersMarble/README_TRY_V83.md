# ComePlayers Marble V83

V83 is a full-project Board Director & City Life update built from V82.

## Open correctly
1. Extract into a new empty folder.
2. Do not overwrite an older project folder.
3. Remove `library`, `temp`, `build`, `.creator`, and `node_modules` if copied from another folder.
4. Open using Cocos Creator 3.8.8 and wait for asset import to finish.
5. Confirm the lobby shows `CINEMATIC CLIENT V83` and `V83 BOARD DIRECTOR & CITY LIFE`.

## Visible V83 checks
- Opening camera sweeps around the board before the countdown.
- Four illuminated billboards surround the arena.
- Property tiles show economy pylons for price tiers.
- Tourism tiles have rotating gold crowns.
- Hotels have illuminated marquees and landmarks have rotating halo rings.
- The active player tile receives camera focus at turn start.
- The top-right rail identifies the leading Monopoly threat and progress percentage.
- Every player card displays a live cash-reserve bar.

V82 save data remains compatible.
