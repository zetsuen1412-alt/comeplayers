# ComePlayers Marble V88 - Fortune Signal Director

1. Extract this full project into a new empty folder.
2. Open it with Cocos Creator 3.8.8.
3. Remove `library`, `temp`, `build`, `node_modules`, and `.creator` if copied from another workspace.
4. Open `assets/scenes/Match.scene` and run Preview.
5. The lobby must show `V88 FORTUNE SIGNAL DIRECTOR`.

## What to test

- Land on a Fortune Signal tile and verify three different cards appear.
- Verify each card shows rarity, title, category, and an effect preview before selection.
- Select one card and confirm the other two choices close before the selected effect resolves.
- Verify persistent rewards update the Fortune status rail: rent shield, free building upgrades, and World Travel tickets.
- Confirm bots choose a card automatically and prefer strategically valuable effects.
- Confirm movement, economic attack, takeover, protection, Island, and World Travel card effects still resolve.
- Confirm the turn does not advance while a human Fortune choice remains open.

V88 preserves V87 development packages, Color Monopoly x2 rent, classic victory conditions, replay, ranked, career, save compatibility, and authoritative online separation.
