Key findings:
- The camera is essentially locked to one full-board isometric pose.
- Normal movement does not trigger close tracking shots.
- Dice are presented in the center without hiding the surrounding board.
- Player panels remain in the four corners.
- The top bar is thin and uses a dark brown/gold client style.
- The board has a dark wood surround, an ivory track, and a gray center.
- Property groups use strong saturated bands.
- Buildings remain compact enough to read from the overview.
- Money stacks are placed around the outside of the track.
- Event popups are small and centered.
- The bottom-center roll control remains the main action target.

V105 response:
- Fixed full-board camera.
- Reduced event zoom and camera movement.
- Video-reference wood/ivory/gray board palette.
- Compact corner HUD and bottom roll dock.
- Simplified route and landing effects.
- Compact property silhouettes.
